# Country photographs and reuse

Country galleries are sourced from each country's English Wikipedia article, selected country-specific landmark articles, and, when needed, country-specific Wikimedia Commons landscape, cityscape, nature, or tourist-attraction categories. Image URLs are returned by Wikimedia's `imageinfo` API, not constructed from guessed filenames.

`src/data/countryGalleries.json` is the per-image attribution manifest. Each entry records the photographer/credit, original Commons description page (`sourceUrl`), verified license name and license URL, original pixel dimensions, caption, local preview, and original image URL. Follow the original description page for complete authorship, provenance, and jurisdiction-specific restrictions. Metadata reflects the source at synchronization time; Commons contributors can subsequently correct it.

Only photographs explicitly marked CC0, public domain, CC BY, or CC BY-SA are accepted. Attribution strings have HTML removed. Display captions omit generic heritage/license templates, residual markup, and repeated language/sentence blocks; clean source-file titles disambiguate duplicate captions. English descriptions are preferred when labeled, without inventing translations. Caption edits leave photographer credits, licensing, source links, and image files unchanged. For CC BY and CC BY-SA, credit the named photographer, link the source and license, and indicate modifications. Local previews are Wikimedia-generated proportional reductions, without cropping or upscaling. Larger previews may additionally receive lossy JPEG recompression with FFmpeg; their pixel dimensions and composition are preserved. CC BY-SA adaptations must retain the applicable share-alike license. Public-domain status may differ by jurisdiction; the Commons source page provides details. These photographs retain their individual licenses independently of the application code.

## Resolution and offline behavior

The bundled collection covers all **194 requested countries** and all **67 existing entries**: **195 countries** including retained Oman, with **1,170 photographs**, six per country. **1,160 originals** meet both the 3,840 × 2,160 4K badge thresholds. Another **1 panorama** is at least 3,840 pixels wide but shorter than 2,160 pixels; **9 originals** are narrower than 3,840 pixels. Every image retains its honest source dimensions. Local previews total approximately **123.4 MiB**, averaging **110.6 KB** per image.

The bounded upgrade pass replaced **47 of the 57** previously non-4K photographs, increasing genuine 4K coverage from **1,113 to 1,160**, while retaining all 1,113 previously qualifying originals and the six-photo country counts. One general discovery pass and one landmark-only follow-up found no suitable additional distinct, openly licensed 4K candidates within the searched sources for the remaining **10** slots: **Comoros (1), Republic of the Congo (1), Djibouti (2), Grenada (2), Nauru (3), and Turkmenistan (1)**. Their existing verified photographs remain intact; these bounded results do not imply that higher-resolution photographs cannot exist elsewhere.

The committed JPEGs in `public/images/countries/` are bounded previews, at most 960 pixels wide and 3 MiB each. They work without a third-party image service or internet connection. `fullUrl` points to the original remote JPEG; its exact original dimensions are `width` and `height`. The UI's 4K badge requires an original at least 3,840 pixels wide and 2,160 pixels high, never an upscaled thumbnail. The synchronizer separately reports originals at least 3,840 pixels wide, including panoramas that do not meet the badge's height threshold. Lower-resolution originals remain labeled with their real dimensions. Original loading requires internet access; applications should retain the local preview as a fallback.

## Rebuilding and verifying

Requires Node.js 22.13+ (Node's built-in TypeScript stripping loads the data-only country catalog). From the project directory:

```powershell
node .\scripts\sync-country-images.mjs
node .\scripts\sync-country-images.mjs --verify
node --test .\scripts\sync-country-images.test.mjs
node .\scripts\sync-country-images.mjs --countries=japan,indonesia
node .\scripts\sync-country-images.mjs --countries=japan --refresh
node .\scripts\sync-country-images.mjs --upgrade
node .\scripts\sync-country-images.mjs --upgrade --landmarks-only
node .\scripts\sync-country-images.mjs --optimize-previews
node .\scripts\sync-country-images.mjs --clean-captions
```

The synchronizer reads `requestedCountries` from `src/data/countryCatalog.ts` and retains Oman from the existing 67-country collection, targets six distinct images per country, prefers genuine originals meeting both 3,840-pixel width and 2,160-pixel height thresholds, checks license metadata, and downloads and validates preview JPEG MIME/signatures/dimensions. Original URLs, MIME types and dimensions are verified through Commons `imageinfo` metadata. Originals are not downloaded or individually HEAD-probed: Wikimedia can rate-limit these large-file requests (HTTP 429), independently of working previews. Source-page links and local previews remain available when originals are temporarily inaccessible. API work is limited to two country workers with throttling and bounded retries. `Retry-After` is honored in both seconds and HTTP-date forms, with a shared per-origin cooldown covering both workers, subsequent files, exhausted retries, and API-level rate-limit responses. Remaining cooldowns longer than 60 seconds defer further requests to that origin rather than retrying early; rerun later to resume. Already complete, locally valid galleries are reused; updates are saved after each photograph so interrupted runs can resume. `--refresh` re-sources the selected galleries. `--upgrade` retains every valid original meeting both 4K thresholds and attempts to replace only deficient slots, including short panoramas. It preserves all existing photographs until a verified, distinct 4K replacement is downloaded; unsuccessful searches or rate limits never discard a working lower-resolution photograph. Only ordinary synchronization uses lower-resolution fallback candidates. Discovery is bounded to the country and listed landmark articles plus four country-specific category families and listed landmark categories; each category traversal visits at most eight categories and stops collecting after reaching 140 files. An incomplete upgrade reports the honest remaining 4K gaps without increasing the six-photo target. `--upgrade --landmarks-only` restricts discovery to the listed landmark categories, avoiding repeated article and broad-category searches during a bounded follow-up pass. `--optimize-previews` requires FFmpeg on PATH and recompresses previews over 100 KiB with two bounded workers, retaining the original whenever recompression is not smaller or changes dimensions. Previously FFmpeg-encoded previews are skipped to avoid repeated lossy encoding. Source originals and attribution are unchanged. `--clean-captions` updates already saved captions entirely offline, without re-sourcing images; ordinary sync also applies the same sanitation to reused and new entries. `--verify` performs offline file and manifest validation, including duplicate-source and SHA-256 duplicate-file checks within each country, and reports counts, genuine 4K totals, and shortfalls without downloading. A country shortfall sets a nonzero exit status rather than inventing, duplicating, or falsely labeling images.
